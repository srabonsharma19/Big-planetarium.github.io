About This Website

Welcome to our Solar System Experience. We’re a Bristol-based planetarium, and we love space! We are predominantly an educational organisation, offering educational entertainment for the public and for schools. We created this project to make space feel closer, clearer, and more exciting. Our goal is simple: help you explore every planet, moon, and mystery in a clean, modern, and visually stunning way. Here, you can scroll through planets one by one, learn their stories, and see the beauty of our cosmic neighborhood. From the blazing Sun to the icy edges of Neptune, every page is designed to feel immersive and easy to understand.

What We Do Present space facts in a simple, modern style Use cinematic visuals to make learning fun Create smooth scrolling pages for each planet Keep everything clean, readable, and beginner‑friendly Our Mission To inspire curiosity. To make learning about space enjoyable. To help everyone understand the universe — one planet at a time.

Why This Project Exists Space is huge, but learning about it shouldn’t be confusing. This website is built for students, explorers, and anyone who loves the stars. We believe knowledge should be beautiful, simple, and accessible. We want the site to be accessible to as many people as possible, in the hopes that anyone can learn more about outer space and get excited like we do. It is important to us that our materials be fun, engaging, and interesting – but for as many people as possible. We don’t want anyone to feel as though science and other complicated topics “aren’t for them”.
